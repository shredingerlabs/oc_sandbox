# PKM as a project kind with a pinned dedicated edition

The Personal Knowledge Wiki (PKM) ships as a third `project_kind` (next to `code` and `chat`) *and* as its own container edition `opencode-sandbox-pkm` (a `FROM opencode-sandbox-base` stage with only the missing `ENTRYPOINT`, no extra installs). At creation, kind `pkm` hides the Edition row and pins `container_edition: "pkm"`; the kind, not the edition, is the single selector.

## Amendment (2026-11-27): the pin is now the pkm bundle's empty set

Under toolchain sets (ADR 0042) the pkm "edition" is the pkm bundle — an empty member list, base-only, like chat. At creation, kind `pkm` hides the bundle/toolchain rows and pins `toolchains: []`; un-pinning the kind restores the *prior* selection (today's hard-code-back to swdev dies). The pkm-specific `ENTRYPOINT` is gone as a concept — the composition footer (`USER dev` + `ENTRYPOINT ["/bin/bash","-l"]`, ADR 0043) is uniform across every set image, base-only sets included. The two-axis rationale (kind recorded once, seeding at creation only) and both-directions switches stand unchanged; the kind, not the set, remains the single selector. We chose the two-axis design — instead of edition-only — because the PKM templates (AGENTS.md wiki schema, agents/commands/skills, `default_agent` + `permissions` in opencode.json, vault skeleton) are seeded at creation time and must survive later edition switches that the revisit sequence permits; recording the kind keeps that seeding explainable even after a user switches the edition away. Templates are seeded only at creation — a later switch to the pkm edition yields the image but no re-seeding.
