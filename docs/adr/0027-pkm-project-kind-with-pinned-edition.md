# PKM as a project kind with a pinned dedicated edition

The Personal Knowledge Wiki (PKM) ships as a third `project_kind` (next to `code` and `chat`) *and* as its own container edition `opencode-sandbox-pkm` (a `FROM opencode-sandbox-base` stage with only the missing `ENTRYPOINT`, no extra installs). At creation, kind `pkm` hides the Edition row and pins `container_edition: "pkm"`; the kind, not the edition, is the single selector. We chose the two-axis design — instead of edition-only — because the PKM templates (AGENTS.md wiki schema, agents/commands/skills, `default_agent` + `permissions` in opencode.json, vault skeleton) are seeded at creation time and must survive later edition switches that the revisit sequence permits; recording the kind keeps that seeding explainable even after a user switches the edition away. Templates are seeded only at creation — a later switch to the pkm edition yields the image but no re-seeding.
